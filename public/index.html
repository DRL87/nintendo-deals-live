What this page is

A browsable shortlist of Nintendo Switch AU eShop Current Offers, filtered to games that are also well-reviewed on Steam. Skim deals on phone or desktop without digging through the store.

How the data was put together

  • Daily scrape of the Australian Nintendo store Current Offers page (GitHub Actions + Chrome — not Cloudflare Workers).
  • Matched each title to Steam, then kept only games rated Overwhelmingly Positive or Very Positive.
  • Prices, discounts, and “Nintendo AU” links come from the AU eShop. Steam % / review counts are a quality filter, not a Nintendo score.
  • Steam genres and tags are shown on each card. Use the Roguelike / lite filters if you want those games only.

How to use it

  • Tap Search & filters (triangle) for search, sort, Steam rating, rogue filters, and genres.
  • Rating and rogue filters stack. Sort reorders the whole list.
  • Open a title or Nintendo AU for the live store page. Prices can change after this snapshot.
